My own quesadilla recipe.

This quesadilla recipe involves just throwing anything into a tortilla
while it fries in a pan.

Ingredients

  • Large tortillas
  • Shredded mexican cheese
  • Tomatoes
  • Onion
  • Lettuce
  • Sour cream
  • Ground meat

Steps

  1. Pan fry ground meat (my choice is turkey).
  2. Move ground meat to a bowl and throw in onions and tomatoes. Avoid crowding the pan to prevent the veggies from steaming rather than frying.
  3. After frying everything, slice a tortilla down the middle halfway through the tortilla.
  4. Place the tortilla in the pan along with ingredients in each section of the tortilla.
  5. Fold the tortilla over itself in fourths.
  6. Take the quesadilla out and put some sour cream on the side. Enjoy!
